## TYPOGRAPHY

### Font Families
- **Primary:** Inter (sans-serif)
  - Used for body text, descriptions
  - Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700

- **Secondary:** Poppins (sans-serif)
  - Used for headings, bold statements
  - Weights: 600, 700, 800

- **Fallback:** SF Pro Display (Apple ecosystem)

### Type Scale

#### Display/Hero
- **Display 1:** 48px, 700 weight, line-height 1.2

## ANIMATION GUIDELINES

### Timing
- **Quick interactions:** 150ms (buttons, icons)
- **Page transitions:** 300ms
- **Modals/overlays:** 250ms
- **Complex animations:** 400-500ms

### Easing
- **Default:** cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)
- **Entrance:** cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)
- **Exit:** cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.6, 1)

### Motion Principles
- Smooth but subtle
- Premium transitions
- Minimal, purposeful motion
- No excessive effects
- Focus on usability

## LOGO USAGE

### App Icon
- **Rounded Square:** 24pt corner radius
- **Background:** Electric Blue (#2563EB)
- **Icon:** White (location pin + home symbol)
- **Size Variants:**
  - iOS: 180x180px (1x), 120x120px (iPad)
  - Android: 192x192px (xxxhdpi), 144x144px (xxhdpi)
  - Web: 512x512px

### Logo with Text
- **Logo + Wordmark**
  - Logo width: 1:1 ratio
  - Wordmark: Poppins Bold
  - "Local" in Dark Navy (#1F2937)
  - "Mind" in Electric Blue (#2563EB)
  - Minimum space: 16px between logo and text

### Clear Space
- Minimum 8px clear space around logo
- Don't overlap or crowd logo
